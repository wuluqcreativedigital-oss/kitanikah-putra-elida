# Music Setup

To add background music to your wedding invitation:

1. Add your wedding song file as 'wedding-song.mp3' in this folder
2. The music will auto-play when guests open the invitation
3. Guests can control music using the floating button in bottom-right corner

Supported formats: MP3, WAV, OGG

The floating music button will:
- Show a pulsing animation
- Turn dark when music is playing
- Allow guests to pause/resume music
- Show hover effects for better UX
